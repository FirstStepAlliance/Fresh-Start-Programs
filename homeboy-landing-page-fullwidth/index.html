Why Financial Empowerment Matters

Your financial well-being plays a big part in building a stronger future.

That's why Homeboy has partnered with First Step Alliance—a nonprofit focused on financial empowerment for justice-involved individuals—to provide free financial education and one-on-one coaching. Together, we're helping our trainees, graduates, and staff gain the knowledge, confidence, and skills to make informed decisions and take control of their finances.

What You'll Learn

  • Budgeting & Saving Strategies: Strengthen your money management skills.
  • Credit Building: Understand how credit works and explore strategies to improve your credit score.
  • Debt Resolution: Learn about your options for reducing and paying off debt, from debt management to debt settlement.
  • Identity Theft Protection: Protect your personal information and know what to do if your identity is compromised.
  • Financial Goal Setting: Set realistic short- and long-term financial goals and create a plan to achieve them.

What's Included

  • Self-paced online courses with certificates of completion
  • Personalized financial coaching from certified credit and housing counselors
  • Podcasts, webinars, e-books, and interactive learning tools
  • Practical resources and worksheets you can use immediately
  • Coaching and educational content available in English and Spanish
